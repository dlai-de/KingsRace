    How to Play

    • Play vs Computer, or name up to 4 friends — one King each.
    • Cards are drawn one by one from the deck.
    • A card's suit moves that King up one row.
    • Kings only move within their own column.
    • When all 4 Kings pass a row, its hidden card flips.
    • An Ace there sends that King one row further.
    • A Joker sends both same-colored Kings back a row.
    • First King to reach the top row wins!
    • If the deck runs out, the King furthest ahead takes it — photo finish.
    • Hit Odds to see each King’s live win chance.
