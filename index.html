    How to Play

    The race

    Four Kings climb four columns. The first King to reach the top row wins.

    Each player rides one King: play against the computer, or name up to 4 friends and let the cards decide between you.

    Cards move Kings

    Cards come off the deck one at a time. The suit says who moves — and a King only ever moves in its own column.

    9♦ is drawn → the King of Diamonds climbs one row. Nobody else moves.

    The hidden row cards

    Every row holds one face-down card. It flips as soon as all four Kings are past that row.

    A♠ flips → the King of Spades climbs one extra row.

    A Joker flips → both Kings of that colour drop one row.

    Winning

    Top row first, and it's over. If the deck runs out before anyone gets there, whoever is furthest up wins.

    Press Odds any time to see each King's chance of winning from where they stand.

    Chips and blinds

    Against the computer you also bet chips on your own King, poker style. Two seats pay the forced bets — the small and big blind — before every hand, and that duty moves one seat round the table each hand.

    Blinds 5/10: one seat puts in 5, the next puts in 10, before a single card is dealt.

    The blinds double every 2 minutes of play (the clock stops while you're paused). Can't cover the big blind, and you're out.

    Your hole cards

    You get 2 secret cards at the start and 1 more before each later betting round. They never race — they show you what isn't coming.

    You hold K♥ 4♥ → two Heart cards that can never be drawn, so the King of Hearts has two fewer climbs left in the deck.

    The betting rounds

    Four of them: before the race starts, then after the 2nd, 4th and 6th hidden card flips.

    On your turn: check, bet, call, raise (3 raises per round) or fold. The blinds already count as a bet, so you may fold straight away in the first round.

    Blinds are 5/10 and you raise to 20. A rider makes it 30 — you can fold, call the 30, or raise again.

    Bet size, all in, folding

    The Bet size slider picks how much you bet or raise, from the minimum up to the round's limit or your last chip. A raise is never smaller than the raise it answers.

    All in keeps you in the hand with your last chips. You can only win from each rider as much as you put in yourself — the rest goes to the next King home.

    Fold and your King keeps racing but can no longer win: the race runs on until a King still in the pot gets home, and its rider takes the pot.
