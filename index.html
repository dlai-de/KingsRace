    How to Play

    • Play vs Computer, or name up to 4 friends — one King each.
    • Cards are drawn one by one from the deck.
    • A card's suit moves that King up one row.
    • Kings only move within their own column.
    • When all 4 Kings pass a row, its hidden card flips.
    • An Ace there sends that King one row further.
    • A Joker sends both same-colored Kings back a row.
    • First King to reach the top row wins!
    • If the deck runs out, the King furthest ahead takes it — photo finish.
    • Hit Odds to see each King’s live win chance.
    • Vs Computer: you and three AI riders each take a King. Two seats post a small and big blind, and the button moves one seat every hand.
    • You’re each dealt 2 hole cards to start and 1 more before every later betting round — dead cards that never race. Yours are secret, and every one of them is an advance its suit will never get.
    • Four betting rounds: before the start, then after the 2nd, 4th and 6th bonus card flips.
    • Check, bet, call, raise (3 max) or fold. The blinds are a live bet, so you can fold straight away on the opening round.
    • Slide Bet size to pick what you bet or raise by — from the minimum up to the street's limit (what a fully raised street would have cost) or your whole stack, whichever comes first. A raise is never smaller than the raise it answers.
    • All in: put your last chips in and you stay in the hand. You can only win from each rider as much as you staked yourself — anything above that goes to the next King home.
    • Blinds go up every 2 minutes of play — the clock stops while you're paused. Can't cover the big blind and you're out.
    • Fold and your King races on, but it can no longer win: it parks at the finish and the race runs until a King still in the pot gets home.
    • The pot goes to that King's rider.
